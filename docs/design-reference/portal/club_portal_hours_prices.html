How 90-Minute Sessions Are Calculated

Example data

Padel matches commonly run for 90 minutes (1.5x hourly rate). When a match crosses two different price bands (e.g., 3:30 PM to 5:00 PM crossing off-peak into prime), rates are prorated to the exact minute.

Tuesday Session 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM (90 mins)

Breakdown: 30 mins Off-Peak (GH₵ 85/hr = GH₵ 42.50) + 60 mins Prime Evening (GH₵ 140/hr = GH₵ 140.00)

Total Court Price GH₵ 182.50 or GH₵ 45.63 per player (Americano of 4)
