Terms of Service

1.2 Service Description

Padel Ghana provides an athletic reservation and live event coordination platform enabling sports enthusiasts across Accra to discover padel facilities, register for organized match formats (including Americano and Mexicano formats), view dynamic court schedules, submit scores, and track live leaderboards.

1.3 Event Participation & Player Responsibilities

Participants enrolled in club sessions must maintain orderly athletic conduct and observe the following responsibilities:

  • check_circle Prompt Arrival: Arrive at designated Accra host facilities at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled round-robin draw.
  • check_circle Safety & Attire: Adhere strictly to court footwear rules (non-marking turf or padel soles) and safety glass regulations.
  • check_circle Sportsmanship: Maintain respectful interactions with fellow participants, ball crew, and club personnel.

1.4 Organiser Roles & Court Authority

All platform sessions are coordinated on-ground by accredited venue directors. Designated organisers reserve full authority to manage player rotations, resolve scoring disagreements, and verify spot validation upon arrival. Organiser determinations regarding court conditions and match seeding are final.

SECTION 02

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

2.1 Data Controller Information

Padel Ghana acts as the Data Controller under the statutory mandates of the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Parliament of Ghana. We are officially recorded with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) Ghana under registration identifier: [Data Protection Commission registration number].

2.2 Categories of Personal Data Collected

Direct Identification

Full legal or sporting name, registered Ghanaian mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact, and optional self-declared padel skill tier (1.0 to 7.0 scale).

Transaction Metadata

Mobile Money network transaction reference IDs (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), event confirmation timestamps, and cash receipts.

2.3 Purpose of Processing

All data collected is processed strictly to automate balanced Americano/Mexicano pairing algorithms, generate real-time leaderboards, dispatch session confirmations, verify on-arrival registrations, and maintain financial records compliant with Ghanaian tax and gaming governance.

2.4 Data Sharing & Clubhouse Displays

During active events, registered player names and active game tallies are publicly rendered on clubhouse TV displays and live digital leaderboards within participating Accra clubs. Padel Ghana does not trade, rent, or sell user personal records to external advertisers or unsolicited third-party promoters.

2.5 Your Statutory Rights

In compliance with Act 843, players retain the explicit right to inspect, correct, obtain copies of, or petition for permanent deletion of their personal and historical match records. Inquiries must be directed in writing to our Data Protection Officer at: [Email].

SECTION 03

Refund & Cancellation Policy

3.1 Player Cancellations (12-Hour Grace Period)

Cancellations confirmed at least 12 hours prior to the scheduled match or event start time are eligible for a 100% platform session credit or a direct monetary reversal to the original source of payment (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or registered bank card).

3.2 Late Cancellations & No-Shows

Because Americano and Mexicano tournament brackets strictly require exact 4-player court sets, cancellations occurring under 12 hours from event start time or unannounced no-shows are non-refundable. Forfeited fees preserve partner balance and compensate standby reserve players.

3.3 Weather Cancellations & Venue Disruptions

In the event of heavy tropical rain, power outages, or unplanned court maintenance across outdoor facilities in Accra, the venue coordinator may postpone or suspend play. In all such weather events, affected participants will automatically receive an unconditional 100% session credit voucher or immediate reschedule slot.

3.4 Refund Processing Timelines

Eligible mobile money refunds will be remitted back to the originating mobile money wallet (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money) within 48 business hours following confirmation by our finance team. Cash settlements require in-person verification at the club pro-shop.

SECTION 04

Platform Code of Conduct

Padel Ghana is committed to fostering an inclusive, high-standard athletic and social ecosystem across the Greater Accra community. Every player, coach, and guest is required to maintain the highest levels of mutual respect, fair play, and court integrity.

Repeated unsportsmanlike behavior, verbal aggression, racket abuse against safety glass panels, or deliberate manipulation of match score entries will result in immediate suspension from future event registrations and potential club ground ban.
