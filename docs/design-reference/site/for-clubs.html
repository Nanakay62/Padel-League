COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick FAQ for club managers

Answers to technical, payment, and operational questions from venue hosts across Accra.

Do we need special hardware or dedicated tablets? devices

No. Padel Ghana is a web-based application engineered to run smoothly on any existing smartphone, iPad, Android tablet, laptop, or smart TV web browser. There are no app store downloads or proprietary terminals required.

How do player payments work? account_balance_wallet

Players pay directly to your club's own MTN MoMo merchant paycode, Telecel Cash, or hand cash to your desk receptionist. When players RSVP, they enter their transaction reference, allowing your court manager to check it off with a single click.

Can we run private member sessions or only public mixers? lock

You have full control. You can generate unlisted, link-only private sessions strictly for your existing club members, or list public Americanos on the Padel Ghana directory to welcome new players into your venue.

What happens if the internet connection cuts out during a tournament? cloud_off

Our courtside scoring tool caches all entries locally on the browser device. The session continues without interruption; as soon as data returns or you reconnect to Wi-Fi, rankings and rounds auto-sync immediately.
