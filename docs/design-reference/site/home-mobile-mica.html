What is Americano?

Rotate partners every round, score individually, everyone plays with everyone, fair for all levels.

Questions

FAQ

How do I join an event? expand_more

Browse the schedule, choose your club and skill division, tap Join, and confirm your payment method.

How do I pay? expand_more

You can pay via Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AT) during checkout or choose cash on-site if permitted by the venue organiser.

What is Americano? expand_more

A dynamic social tournament where you rotate partners every match round while accumulating individual points on a unified leaderboard.

What do the levels mean? expand_more

Levels range from Beginner (learning basic racket positions) to Advanced (consistent glass usage, overheads, and competitive matchplay).
