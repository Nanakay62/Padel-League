Playing & Formats

Rules, round rotations, skill tiers, and single registrations.

How do I find and join an event? expand_more
Open the platform, browse events at Accra clubs, select your level, and reserve your spot. You will receive an immediate confirmation with court details, schedule overview, and direct WhatsApp contact for your event host.
What is the Americano format? expand_more
A social match play format where you enter as a solo player, rotate partners each round, and play with and against everyone. Individual points accumulate into a single leaderboard over approximately 90 to 120 minutes of fast-paced padel.
What is the Mexicano format? expand_more
A competitive format coming soon where round 1 is random, and subsequent pairings are dynamically re-seeded from the live leaderboard so top players face each other, ensuring closely contested, exciting sets every round.
What skill level should I select? expand_more
Levels range from Level 1 for beginners learning padel wall rebounds up to Level 5 for seasoned racket players. Organisers specify the rating bracket for each session to guarantee balanced matches across all four courts.
Do I need a fixed partner to play? expand_more
No, in Americano and Mexicano formats, you register individually and rotate partners every round. It is the quickest, most welcoming way to connect with the local Accra sporting community.

Payments & Mobile Money

Local currency, MoMo gateways, cash handling, and cancellation terms.

How do I pay for a session? expand_more
You can pay via local mobile money — MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money — or cash at the club reception desk. The organiser verifies your spot upon confirmation and your receipt is immediately accessible in your player portal.
Can I pay with cash on arrival? expand_more
Yes, select cash during RSVP and pay the organiser at the club desk before warmups. Please arrive 15 minutes before court start time so organizers can settle the session roster smoothly.
What is the refund and cancellation policy? expand_more
Cancellations made at least 12 hours before event start are eligible for full credit or refund according to club policies. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for specific details regarding inclement tropical weather reschedules.

Clubs & Venues

Accra locations, gear rental, locker facilities, and parking.

Where are the padel courts located in Accra? expand_more
Padel courts are situated across key Accra hubs including Cantonments, Airport Residential, Labone, and East Legon. All partnered facilities offer floodlit panoramic turf courts, changing facilities, and dedicated player lounges.
Can I rent a padel racket and balls at the court? expand_more
Yes, participating Accra venues provide rental rackets and padel balls courtside. Premium carbon rackets can be rented for GHS 50–70 per session, with balls provided complimentary for all Americano fixtures.

App & Clubhouse TV

Browser-first PWA, TV leaderboards, offline courtside scoring.

Do I need to download an app from an app store? expand_more
No, Padel Ghana is a fast mobile web app. Open padelghana.com in your phone browser and tap 'Add to Home Screen' for instant 1-tap access with zero storage footprint and immediate push notices.
How does courtside live scoring work? expand_more
Players or court captains tap simple plus and minus buttons after each game. Scores sync directly to the clubhouse TV leaderboard and work even when network connection drops, queuing updates locally until connection restores.
