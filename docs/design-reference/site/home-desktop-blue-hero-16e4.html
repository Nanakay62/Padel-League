Rotate partners every round, score individually, everyone plays with everyone, fair for all levels.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I join an event?keyboard_arrow_down

Select any upcoming tournament, social, or Americano from the club schedule. Hit join, choose your payment method, and you're in the draw once approved by the organizer.

How do I pay?keyboard_arrow_down

Mobile money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money) or cash at the club; organiser confirms your spot. Note online card payments are not live yet.

What is Americano?keyboard_arrow_down

Americano is a social tournament format where you rotate partners every round. Everyone plays with everyone and against everyone. Points won in each game accumulate on your personal score sheet.

What do the levels mean?keyboard_arrow_down

Events are classified from Level 1 (beginners familiarizing with wall rebounds) up to Level 5 (advanced tournament players). Organisers outline the intended rating bracket so all matches stay competitive.
